Acme Corporation Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager
approval.

Employees working remotely must use the company VPN when accessing
internal systems. Multi-factor authentication is required for all
remote access.

Company confidential information must not be stored on personal
devices. Security incidents must be reported to the security team
within one hour of discovery.

Full-time employees receive twenty paid vacation days per calendar
year. Up to five unused vacation days may be carried into the next
calendar year.
